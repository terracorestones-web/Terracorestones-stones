About Mosaic from India

Stone mosaic — small pieces of natural stone arranged into patterns — adds detail and craft to floors, walls and feature areas. Terracore Stones coordinates stone mosaic in India, matching your design on colour, pattern and finish. We audit the fabricator, enforce quality through production, and personally sign off every piece before export, so the pattern arrives exactly as designed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Terracore Stones actually do?

Terracore Stones is a buyer's agent inside India. We are not a trader and not a middleman — we are engaged directly by international buyers to act as their own presence in India, paid for consultancy, QA and logistics, never for the material. We audit quarries and factories, enforce production QA, sign off containers, and handle export logistics.

How does Terracore Stones make money if it charges no markup on material?

We are paid a consultancy fee for our services — supplier and quarry audits, production QA, container sign-off, and export logistics. We never take a markup on the material itself, which means our incentive is aligned with the buyer: we are paid to protect your interests, not to sell you more stone.

Which stones can Terracore Stones source?

We source marble, granite, limestone, basalt and other natural stones across India's major quarry belts, as well as fully custom cuts, finishes, fluted panels, furniture, inlay work, outdoor cladding, pool and deck stone, wall panels and mosaic.

Do you work with architects, interior designers and developers?

Yes. We support architects, designers and developers in India, Mauritius, and are expanding to Australia and the UAE. We act as your operational presence on the ground, so you can source Indian stone with confidence without being there yourself.

How do you ensure quality on a stone order?

We audit quarries and factories against your specification, inspect raw blocks for hidden flaws before a single cut, enforce dimensional tolerances and shade/vein consistency via dry-lays during production, and personally inspect every container before it is sealed.

Do you handle shipping and export logistics?

Yes. We manage documentation, ISPM 15 compliance and port coordination — from the factory floor to your destination port — so your material arrives on time and in spec.
