# Terracore Stones — Pricing & Engagement Model

> Terracore Stones is a buyer's agent inside India for natural stone procurement, QA and logistics. We are **not a trader and not a middleman** — we are paid for consultancy, QA, and logistics, never for the material.

## Engagement model

- **No markup on material.** We are paid a consultancy fee for our services, so our incentive is aligned with the buyer, not the supplier.
- **You own the deal with your supplier.** We own everything that happens on the ground between the quarry and your container.
- **No minimum order.** We work with single-slab samples through full container loads.

## Services

| Service | What it covers |
|---------|----------------|
| Supplier & quarry audits | Vet quarries and factories against your spec; inspect raw blocks for hidden flaws before cutting |
| Production QA | On-ground during your run; dimensional tolerances, shade & vein consistency via dry-lays |
| Container sign-off | Personal inspection of packing, lashing, and aesthetic uniformity before sealing |
| Export logistics | Documentation, ISPM 15 compliance, port coordination — factory floor to destination port |

## Materials we source

- Marble, granite, limestone, basalt
- Custom fluted finish, custom furniture, custom inlay work
- Outdoor & cladding, pool & deck, wall panels, mosaic
